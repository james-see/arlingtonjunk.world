Common questions

How much does junk removal cost?

Single items start at $99. Send a photo for a flat upfront price — most full loads run a few hundred dollars depending on volume.

How fast can you come?

Same-week pickup in most cases, and we’re open 24 hours for bookings. Call or text (571) 516-6949 anytime and we’ll find the earliest slot.

Do I need to move everything to the curb?

No. Point at it where it sits — we do all the lifting, loading, and sweeping up.

What won’t you take?

Hazardous materials like paint, chemicals, fuel, and asbestos. Almost everything else is fair game — just ask.

What happens to my junk?

We donate what we can, recycle what we can’t donate, and dispose of the rest responsibly.

Do you serve my neighborhood?

We’re based in Arlington and serve all of Northern Virginia — Arlington, Fairfax, and Loudoun counties plus Alexandria — as well as Washington, DC and Potomac, Maryland.
